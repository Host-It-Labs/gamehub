# Yatai — Lantern Festival

An optional expansion with two components: customer orders and specialty stalls. Enable it in solo setup or the online lobby. All players use the same expansion. Existing base-game saves stay base games.

## Customer orders

With the first dish of each round, choose one of three customer orders. Your choice locks privately alongside your dish and is revealed when all players have chosen. Everyone may choose the same order: there is no first-player race.

| Round | Order | Required dishes | Bonus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Tea for two | 2 Moon bun + 1 Lantern tea | 7 |
| 1 | Sweet escape | 2 Berry fizz + 1 Cloud cake | 7 |
| 1 | Street feast | 2 Star skewer + 1 Dumpling | 7 |
| 2 | Midnight picnic | 1 Moon bun + 2 Dumpling | 7 |
| 2 | Lantern date | 1 Cloud cake + 2 Lantern tea | 7 |
| 2 | Festival flight | 1 Berry fizz + 2 Star skewer | 7 |

Only the six dishes drafted in that order's round count, including the first pick. Extra dishes do not multiply the reward. Completed orders add 7 points; unfinished orders add zero and carry no penalty. Dishes remain on your board and retain all normal food scoring. Round-one orders close when round two starts; round-two dishes cannot finish them.

## Specialty stalls

Start with two permits for the entire game. Before picking a dish, toggle **Open a stall** to spend one permit and open a stall for that dish type. The action is part of your normal pick and reveals simultaneously. You may cancel the toggle before playing.

Every later dish of that type earns 2 bonus points, capped at 6 per stall. Neither the opening dish nor previously collected dishes earn stall points. Stalls persist across the two rounds. You may open at most two stalls, and they must be different dish types. You may pick those dishes normally without spending a permit.

Example: draft a Moon bun and open its stall. Your next three buns each earn 2 stall points; further buns add no stall points. All buns still score their normal pairs.

## Scoring and strategy

Final score = normal food score + completed customer orders + stall bonuses. The expansion can add at most 26 points. The live festival strip shows order requirements and progress, stall bonuses, and remaining permits. Inspect another player's board to see their revealed festival progress.

Orders create short-term drafting goals; stalls create investments across rounds. The opening pick earns no immediate stall bonus, so build around dishes you expect to see again. Customers can support a stall plan or pull you toward a different dish. Neither component changes the deck, hand size, tea competition, pick count, or passing direction.

Bots use only their observation and remembered packets. Medium bots value order progress and future stall opportunities; hard bots simulate expansion scoring. No extra phase or turn is introduced.


Menu variety: new matches randomly draw six distinct menus from a 26-menu catalogue and offer three per round. Offers are saved with the match and shared by all players. Existing saves without offers keep the original sets. Chosen menus stay visible as illustrated toolbar controls; specialty permits and bonus points appear on their dishes. Neighbour previews put chosen menus above the unchanged board.
