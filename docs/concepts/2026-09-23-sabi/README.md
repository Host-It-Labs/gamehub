# Sabi artwork, 23 September 2026

Party game `miro` (formerly Mundo), provisional public name **Sabi**. Art direction: "the measuring desk" (green self-healing cutting mat, cream cards, glossy enamel tokens, warm lamp from upper left). Generated with Codex CLI `$imagegen`, one image per invocation, all launched in parallel, fresh text-only prompts, no reference images. Each prompt below was sent verbatim after the wrapper `$imagegen Generate this image using the built-in image generation tool. Save the final image to the exact absolute path: <path>. Prompt: `.

## Prompt: cover

```text
Use case: square library box cover illustration for a party trivia game. Newest image model, highest quality, largest native square size, PNG, no watermark.

Style: crisp stylised 3D tabletop illustration with soft clay-and-enamel toy materials, clean rounded shapes, bright and optimistic, sleek rather than busy. The setting is "the measuring desk": a modern, bright, playful trivia desk. The whole surface is a deep green self-healing cutting mat (#1f5a4a with darker #174537 areas) printed with a fine pale grid and small ruler ticks along its edges. One warm desk-lamp light comes from the upper left and casts soft, real, slightly long shadows to the lower right. Palette: cutting-mat green #1f5a4a and #174537, pale mint-grey grid lines, cream #fbf6e9, signal tomato-red #ff5a3c, cobalt blue #3569ff, sunflower yellow #ffd23f, with small brass accents.

Composition: a top-down, slightly angled view of the green cutting mat, which fills the entire square to every edge (full bleed, the mat continues past all four edges, no table edge, no border, no frame, no background outside the mat). Scattered around the outer ring of the square, leaving the centre open: a small glossy desk globe with one tomato-red enamel pin stuck in it; a brass magnifying glass; a yellow tape measure with its tape partly pulled out; a cobalt stopwatch; a cream lightbulb; a few chunky glossy enamel tokens shaped like a question mark and an exclamation mark in red, cobalt and yellow; two flat cream index cards, one showing a solid black elephant silhouette and one showing a solid black tall tower silhouette. Objects are cropped naturally by the edges in places so the scene feels continuous.

Title: across the centre, the single word "Sabi" (capital S, then lowercase a, b, i), spelled exactly S-a-b-i, as huge chunky rounded glossy enamel letters in cream #fbf6e9 with a tomato-red #ff5a3c edge and a soft cast shadow on the mat, lying flat on the desk and slightly tilted, custom friendly typography, perfectly legible even as a small thumbnail. The title sits entirely within the central 84% of the square with clear margin around it and dominates the composition.

Must not appear: any other text, letters, numbers or digits anywhere (the tape measure, ruler ticks, stopwatch and grid carry no numerals or words), no logos, no user interface, no screenshot, no box mockup, no border, no frame, no vignette card, no gray mat, no people or hands. Not neon or night glass, not a gouache travel poster, not cut paper or kraft, not ink printmaking or risograph.
```
