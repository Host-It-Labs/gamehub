# Impostor and Diner covers, 3 October 2026

Library square covers and wide setup-lid covers for two new games: Impostor
(`impostor`, online drawing party game: a detective's evidence board at night,
cat sketches and one fox) and Diner (`diner`, real-time solo/co-op: a 1950s
roadside diner at night with a mimic among three matching guests).

## Generation

Fresh text-only generations, one image per Codex invocation, two variants
(`-a`, `-b`) per image, all eight launched in parallel through `gen.sh`. No
reference images, no edits. Model `gpt-6-luna` (newest Luna from
`.claude/skills/artwork/newest-luna.mjs`), low reasoning effort, codex-cli
0.157.1, stdin closed. `gen.sh` takes the PNG from the run's own
`~/.codex/generated_images/<session id>/` folder, requires exactly one PNG
there and checks the saved copy by sha256. Logs are in `logs/`.

Outputs (generated into `public/art/`, then moved with the other cover
sources to the art archive beside the repo, `../gamehub-art-archive/public/art/`):
`box-impostor-v1-{a,b}.png`, `box-impostor-wide-v1-{a,b}.png`,
`box-diner-v1-{a,b}.png`, `box-diner-wide-v1-{a,b}.png`. The wide images are
cut to 3:1 keeping their middle band (y 256–768 of 1024).

## Round 2 prompts

Two images failed round 1 for reasons a retry of the same text would likely
repeat, so their prompts gained one instruction each (everything else is
unchanged) and were generated fresh, two variants each.

- `prompts/impostor-square-v2.txt`: both v1 squares came back as RGBA with a
  soft fade to transparency at every edge (about half the pixels below full
  opacity, the bottom edge almost clear), which breaks full-bleed edge
  coverage. Added before "Artwork fills all four edges": a fully-opaque
  sentence (no alpha, no fade to transparency, the room and board continue
  solidly into all four corners).
- `prompts/diner-wide-v2.txt`: both v1 wides hung the neon sign high on the
  wall, so the 3:1 cut sliced off the top of the "Diner" letters. Added after
  the title-size sentence: strict vertical placement (sign hung low above the
  counter, backplate within 34–66 % and letters within 38–62 % of the height,
  heads no higher than 35 %, feet no lower than 70 %).

## Integration

- `scripts/optimize-box-covers.mjs` (ids `impostor`, `diner`; writes the 1024
  square from the archive PNG, then the spine and 320/640 sizes) and
  `scripts/optimize-wide-covers.mjs` (3:1 middle band at 768/1536). Both take
  optional ids: `node scripts/optimize-box-covers.mjs impostor diner`.
- Served files: `public/art/optimized/box-{impostor,diner}-blind-v1{,-320,-640,-spine}.webp`
  and `box-{impostor,diner}-wide-v1{,-768}.webp`, with preview entries in
  `lib/artwork-previews.json`.
- `coverSlugs` in `lib/games/box-covers.ts`; the cover id list in
  `scripts/production-assets.mjs`; `.dockerignore` regenerated with
  `node scripts/production-assets.mjs --write`.
