# Rota: three towns, 17 October 2026

William rejected the mills and asked for a complete map rework, with ten
rounds on the second map, fourteen on the third, and no fourth map.

Keep the sober northern mill-town world, true vehicle/building scale, ink
edges and material detail. The old scene repeats terraces almost everywhere;
the new maps need different geography and street rhythms at the actual game
camera, without changing the routes' hit areas or covering empty squares.

- Meadow: seven rounds on 7×7. A smaller market town, tree-filled gardens
  between residential blocks, ochre civic paving, warm green cleared land.
  Keep the enclosing terrace ring so only real exits meet the board.
- Harbour: ten rounds on 9×9. Four inner wharves in a stepped eastern dock
  basin. Granite retaining walls, pale coping stones, iron bollards, cranes
  and boats; longer warehouse roofs outside the site. Cool grey yards and
  deep teal water make the docks read as a working port.
- Ridge: fourteen rounds on 10×10. Two broken rock bands frame the civic
  centre; sixteen exits. A compact industrial valley, open pine districts,
  gritstone formations and heather slopes, with routes kept flat.

The mill source is rebuilt separately: structural bays, meaningful roof
profiles, pilasters and sills. Empty mill plots must read as low, orderly
sites instead of a rubble box under a huge chimney. Preserve source models,
brief, actual renders and runtime comparisons before optimizing exports.

Harbour/Ridge layout revisions are versioned. Existing games retain their
original geometry, cell indices and duration, including saved Capital games.
New-game setup offers exactly three maps. Browser checks include empty and
grown maps, phone/tablet/desktop framing, six seats, placement and Undo.
