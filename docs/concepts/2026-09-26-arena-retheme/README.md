# Arena re-theme: two desktop directions (26 September 2026)

William asked for a complete redesign of Arena's artwork and theming, away
from the Clash Royale look, built on a frame that can travel across past,
present and future and across genres (medieval fantasy included). Two
directions, one desktop mockup each (1536x1024), with the real HUD on top.
Concept mockups only: nothing here is integrated.

- **Toybox**: the battle is fought by toys on a playroom floor. Every troop,
  tower and bridge is a toy from a different toy line and era (plastic
  knights, tin robots, rubber dinosaurs, vinyl astronauts), so eras mix for
  free and each arena is a different room or playset.
- **Rift**: every arena is ground torn out of time, fusing two eras into one
  field. The river becomes a glowing tear in time; each half, its towers and
  its troops come from a different era (here: a medieval morning against a
  neon night).

Fixed elements taken from the running game (`docs/arena.md`, Screen): an
18 x 32 field seen from the near end at the desktop camera (38 degrees,
40 degree lens), one dividing channel with two bridges near the flanks, a
king and two small towers a side with health bars, back button top left,
clock with two crown counts and a pause button top right, nothing else at the
top, the hand tray at the bottom centre (next card smaller at the left, four
cards with cost gems, ten-segment energy bar). Digits on the clock, crowns,
costs and health bars are illustrative only.

Generated with `codex exec -m gpt-6-luna` (reasoning effort low), built-in
`$imagegen`, one image per run, fresh from text, no reference images.
