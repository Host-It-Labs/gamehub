# Arena: Collector's Diorama, Medieval board (26 September 2026, night)

William chose the Collector's Diorama direction
(`arena-collector-desktop-v1.png`) and replaced the Toybox bedroom. The
battlefield is a premium collector's diorama: a crafted miniature landscape
on a slim dark graphite plinth, floating in a soft graphite studio void,
lit like a luxury product shot. Each side's half and towers will later take
a genre of their own; for now **both sides are Medieval**, as the near half
of the mockup. The forest and medieval scenery of the first Arena come back
around the field, on the plinth. This round is the board only (field,
towers, bridges, water, walls, forest, plinth, light, HUD); characters come
after William reviews it.

## Look

- **A master model-maker's diorama, photographed.** Realistic miniature
  terrain: sculpted and painted stone, static grass and flock, moss in the
  cracks, weathering and dry-brushed highlights, fine detail at small scale.
  Not cartoon, not hand-painted brush textures, not chunky Clash-style
  props, not toys, not a child's world.
- **Grown-up palette.** Stone greys with blue and warm undertones, deep moss
  and grass greens, dark weathered oak, slate roofs, iron and aged brass.
  Team colour only where heraldry goes: banners, pennants, shield
  emblems (no letters), roof trims: deep cobalt blue for the near side,
  lacquer red for the far side.
- **Light.** One large soft key light from the upper left, a cool rim light
  from behind, crisp soft shadows; the plinth void is graphite to warm grey.

## The board (as in the mockup's near half)

- The field: weathered cobblestone paving with moss and grass in the gaps
  and patches of short grass, lanes to the towers slightly cleaner.
- Along the field's edges: low moss-covered dry-stone walls, a few banners
  on poles at the corners.
- The river: dark glossy water in a channel with stone embankments; two
  stone bridges (an old stone arch, and a timber-decked stone bridge).
- Towers: the king tower is a squat stone keep with a dark slate roof, a
  crenellated wall walk and a big team banner on its front; a small bronze
  cannon on a turntable on its battlements. Small towers are round stone
  watchtowers with an open crenellated top (a guard stands there) and a
  team pennant. Rubble: the same stones tumbled, a torn banner.
- Around the field, on the plinth: forest (pines and oaks), rocks and small
  cliffs, bushes, flowers and grass tufts, stumps, logs, barrels and crates,
  torches, as in the first Arena but at diorama quality. The landscape ends
  at the plinth edge in a clean cut showing the earth and rock layers above
  the graphite base.

## Scale and budgets

One unit is one tile. Towers keep the v1 sizes (king radius 2, about 5 tall;
small towers radius 1.5, about 4.3 tall). High resolution but fast: detail
from geometry where it reads (stone courses, crenels, roof tiles as rows),
from the 2048 px tiles and baked AO elsewhere; instancing for repeated
pieces. Budgets: king ≤ 30k triangles, small tower ≤ 18k, each bridge ≤ 10k,
a wall segment ≤ 1k (it is instanced about seventy times), a tree ≤ 3k.
