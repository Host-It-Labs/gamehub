# Dino and Sumo: quality-first rebuild

The user rejected the 4 October v8 rebuild: Dino read as a large chicken,
and Sumo's inflated body and awkward arms lacked convincing anatomy. The
earlier [figure brief](../2026-10-04-arena-figures/brief.md) and its renders are
historical evidence of the rejected version, not user approval.

The new [high-fidelity-3d skill](/Users/williamguinaudie/.codex/skills/high-fidelity-3d/SKILL.md)
is mandatory for the root and every agent doing 3D work. Gamehub's root
instructions and the user's global agent instructions both require it.
Build and inspect detailed editable masters before making runtime copies.

## Dino

A powerful stylized tyrannosaur with a near-horizontal trunk, a long tapering
rear counterweight, substantial hip and thigh masses, bent knee/ankle structure,
a short strong neck and a deep angular skull. The eyes sit within shaped
reptilian brows and lids, above a substantial muzzle and readable jaw hinge.
Two small two-finger arms, three weight-bearing toes and the tail retain their
live joints. Forest/olive hide has restrained darker dorsal markings, warmer
undersides and scales with a deliberate size hierarchy. Teeth, nostrils,
mouth interior and claws receive authored shapes and distinct surfaces.

Anatomical landmarks were checked against the American Museum of Natural
History's [mounted skeleton](https://www.amnh.org/exhibitions/permanent/saurischian-dinosaurs/tyrannosaurus-rex)
and [anatomy discussion](https://www.amnh.org/explore/ology/paleontology/ask-a-scientist-about-t-rex).
The low head, horizontal balance and rear tail replace the previous upright
tail and rounded chest. This is an original stylized character; it does not
reproduce a particular museum reconstruction.

Building clearance must be solved through actual pose/contact behavior.
Do not fold the tail vertically or compress the anatomy to hide a collision.

## Sumo

A believable powerful adult wrestler with a broad chest and clavicle structure,
deltoids, trapezius and lats, a substantial soft abdomen, a defined waist and
strong proportionate thighs/calves. Shoulder, upper arm, elbow, forearm and
hand must remain connected through palm strikes. Sculpt muscle and soft-tissue
landmarks within coherent surfaces; avoid sausage limbs and exposed rigid cuts.
His face has adult planes, a proportionate head and focused expression. Hair
forms a credible folded topknot; the mawashi has cloth tension, layered folds,
a back knot and restrained sagari detail. Skin, woven cloth and hair need
distinct surfaces that stay stable in Arena's lighting.

The Japan Sumo Association's [photo reports](https://sumo.or.jp/Jungyo/jungyo_report/)
and [wrestler roster](https://sumo.or.jp/ResultRikishiData/search/) provide
structural reference for stance, abdomen and shoulder mass. The character is
an original design rather than a likeness of a real athlete.

## Review and integration

Each author supplies neutral-clay front, side, rear and game-camera views,
anatomy close-ups and representative moving poses. The root inspects those
artifacts and records concrete shortcomings before optimization. Review the
exported models again in Arena at phone, tablet and landscape sizes, including
walking, turns, full attack cycles and building contact. Preserve high-resolution
masters and all rejected sources. Technical tests support this review; they
do not establish artistic acceptance.

The towers, recharging shields, status indicators, drag preview and movement
fixes from the original task remain part of the intended result. New models
must preserve their behavior, with shape-dependent contact tests repeated.
