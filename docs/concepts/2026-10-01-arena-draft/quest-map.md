# Quest map paintings v2 (1 October 2026)

William: the map looks layered on top of its painting, the painting looks
blurry, and the map should feel more animated and more like a game.

## Why the v1 maps looked blurry

- The sources are the image tool's native 1536x1024 (wide) and 1024x1536
  (tall). `scripts/arena-splash.mjs` resized the wide cut _up_ to 1600 and
  saved webp at quality 80, which smeared the painted edges, then the
  browser stretched it again with `object-fit: cover` to fill a 1440x900
  window at device pixel ratio 2 (2880 device pixels across: about 1.9x
  upscaling of an already softened file).
- The v1 prompt asked for the middle 60% to be "calm, open ground with only
  gentle, low-contrast variation". That is exactly what reads as out of
  focus: a big soft mottled field with no crisp edge anywhere, right under
  the stops.
- Every panel over the map (the start pick, rewards, shops) put a
  `backdrop-filter: blur(3px)` on the whole painting.

The v2 prompts below ask for crisp painted detail everywhere, including
the open ground, and a game-map composition: open clearings for the stops
(they are generated per run, so nothing route-like may be painted), the
boss's landmark painted at the top centre where the boss stop always sits,
and the region's scenery framing the sides. Two variants per image, one
image per run, fresh from text; the cut script now keeps the native size
and sharpens a 2x lanczos cut for high-density screens.

Each prompt is sent exactly as written below.
