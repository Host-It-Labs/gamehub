# Mora paper worlds v2 prompts — 19 September 2026

Why v2: the v1 results had the right sanctuary but too little expendable forest
(landscape: thin on every side; portrait: none above the dome), so the runtime
could not zoom or crop freely. These prompts describe the padding as a visible
proportion and a comparison, name the top first, and add a correction pass.
Pads are also requested much larger (about 10% of the sanctuary width), so a
placed creature keeps its tray size. See the skill's "Padding the generator can
actually follow" section.

Margin acceptance (landmark union / source size): landscape ≥ 0.20 per side;
portrait ≥ 0.14 top, ≥ 0.30 bottom, ≥ 0.12 sides. Count 17 pads after every pass.

## Result, 19 September 2026

Both prompts were run as separate fresh generations and integrated as
`mora-paper-world-landscape-v2.png` and `mora-paper-world-portrait-v2.png`.
Generating separately gave more consistent buildings and palette than a portrait
follow-up inside the landscape thread. Pads were auto-detected by colour with
`scripts/measure-pads.mjs` (15/17 landscape, 13/17 portrait; the rest filled from
grid spacing) and are about 60 px native, so a placed creature keeps its tray
size. Margins: landscape covers 1440×900 and larger; portrait covers 375×812 with
no soft copy. Landmarks, pond and crown polygons were estimated by eye and can
be tightened later. Crops are extracted by `scripts/optimize-paper-worlds.mjs`.
