## Viking

A broad adult Norse warrior: weight through hips, solid thighs and calves,
an expressive weathered face, a substantial ginger beard with authored locks
and braids, and a real iron nasal helmet without horns. Layered wool tunic,
restrained grey fur mantle, leather belt and leg wraps have distinct construction
and finish. The two-handed bearded axe has a credible shaft, socket, bevel and
worn edge. Connected shoulders/elbows/wrists and convincing grip must survive
walking, the full circular axe strike, turns and rage. Preserve the readable
stocky silhouette, grounded motion and existing axe/rage timing.

## Gladiator

A compact athletic adult fighter with coherent chest/clavicle/shoulder planes,
tapered limbs, a focused adult face and readable sandal-clad feet. Bronze helmet,
layered leather skirt and greaves show construction, edge thickness and measured
wear. A curved rectangular shield has a boss, rim, straps and a usable grip;
the short sword has a credible guard and blade. Preserve shield recharge,
placement scale and gameplay timing. Shield brace, sword thrust and recovery
must stay connected and clear of his own body and the stone at real play scale.

## Medic

A capable adult field medic with distinctive facial structure and a calm,
focused expression. A shaped cream uniform, green medical emblems, gathered
sleeves, collar, belt, sturdy shoes and practical satchel establish the role
at small scale. Hair and cap show authored construction. Hands, elbows and
shoulders stay connected during walking and healing; healing equipment has
plausible grips and attachment points. Preserve the original healer identity
and timing, with restrained breathing, grounded steps and clear heal/recovery.
