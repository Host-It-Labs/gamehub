Use case: ui-mockup. Create one complete in-game screen of a desktop browser card game, concept art for a new art direction of a trick-taking game called Nox, played at night. Landscape 1536x1024 PNG, largest native landscape size, highest quality, no watermark.

Style, direction ORBIT: confident mid-century modernist flat graphic design, like a 1960s space-age science poster or a vintage airline poster for flights to the moon, all bold circles, arcs and orbits. Everything is built from a small number of large, clean, flat shapes with crisp edges: rectangles, circles, half circles, triangles and long parallel stripes, arranged on a clear grid with generous calm areas. Flat colour only: no brush texture, no paper grain, no ink texture, no painterly shading, no soft gradients, no fine detail. Depth comes only from overlapping flat shapes, flat offset shadows and one translucent flat shape of light. It looks designed, deliberate, witty and calm, like a real printed poster by a great graphic designer, never like a digital painting. Palette, strictly limited: deep space black-violet and a dark teal, warm cream, pale turquoise, tangerine orange as the accent, and a little signal red.

World: a card game played on an orbiting space station above the night side of the Earth, seen straight from above the station. The play surface is a flat rectangular hatch panel on top of the main module: one large rectangle of dark teal, with a slim cream line running around it a little inside its edge; the rim band outside that line is a calm, continuous band of flat cream hull plating on all four sides. Around it, filling the picture to every edge: to the left and to the right, long solar panel wings drawn as rectangles with a grid of thin pale turquoise stripes; at the top, a round docking port as concentric circles and a small cone-shaped capsule docked to it; a small antenna dish as a cream circle; thin cream orbit lines sweeping across the background as large arcs; far below the station, the dark curve of the Earth at night filling the lower corners, dotted with tiny tangerine city lights and edged with a thin pale turquoise line of atmosphere; the rest of the sky deep black-violet with small cream dot stars and one large cream circle of the moon partly cut by the top edge at the upper left. Light: the sun, beyond the upper right edge, is the single light source; its light is one long translucent cream wedge falling across the upper right, and every flat shadow falls toward the lower left.

Layout of the game screen, drawn in this style. The play area is a wide rectangle, face-on and perfectly flat in true plan view, spanning about the middle 72 percent of the image width and running from about 13 percent to about 70 percent of the image height; the scenery surrounds it on every side and fills the picture to every edge. Around the play area runs a calm, continuous rim band, empty so that name tags can sit on it anywhere. Four players sit around the table, each shown only as a compact horizontal pill-shaped seat tag lying on the rim band: a small circular avatar made of a simple flat geometric pattern (no faces), a short name and a small score number. The viewer's tag lies on the bottom rim, clearly left of centre, about one third of the way along from the left end; the others lie on the middle of the left rim, the middle of the top rim and the middle of the right rim. The top rim tag has a bright ring in the accent colour around its avatar, showing that it is that player's turn. Just inside the rim, four small flat chevron arrows, one at the middle of each side, chase each other clockwise around the table: the one on the top side points right, the one on the right side points down, the one on the bottom side points left and the one on the left side points up. In the middle of the play area lies the current trick: three face-up cards lying flat and straight, slightly apart, each one nudged toward the player who played it (left, top and right), each with a flat offset shadow that falls away from the light. No chairs, no seats, no benches, no people and no hands anywhere.

Cards: crisp cream-white cards with small rounded corners and a thin inner border, each with one large bold numeral at the top left in a heavy modernist sans serif and one large flat geometric suit shape in the centre. Five suits, each with its own colour: spades black-violet, hearts signal red, clubs dark teal, diamonds tangerine orange, and a fifth suit, Storm, whose card face is split diagonally into tangerine and black-violet with a bold cream lightning bolt, a solar storm. The storm cards are the dangerous ones.

The hand dock at the bottom, below the play area: a long floating tray with rounded ends in flat black-violet with a thin cream edge, like a strip of station hull, about 60 percent of the image width, resting just above the bottom edge, holding ten overlapping face-up cards in one straight row, each overlapping the next so that its numeral and suit stay visible, one of them a storm card; one card is lifted clear above the others and brought to the front, selected. At the left end of the tray: one flat die drawn as a cream rounded square showing a heart suit shape, and beside it two small round flat tokens, one with a shield shape and the number 2, one with a four-pronged grappling-hook shape. At the right end of the tray: a flat rounded rectangular button in the accent colour with the single word Play, and next to it a small round cream button with a curved undo arrow.

Navigation drawn on top, and nothing else: top-left corner, a small round cream button with a left arrow, and beside it the word Nox in a bold modernist sans serif. Top-right corner, one small round cream menu button with three short horizontal lines. Top centre, one small cream pill showing 2/4. No other interface, no status bar, no captions, no helper text.

Text: the only words in the image are Nox and Play. The only other characters are the card numerals from 1 to 10, the round count 2/4, the small score numbers and the four short names on the seat tags: You, Rook, Static and Moth. No other letters, words, labels, logos, brand names or watermark anywhere, not on the station, the panels, the capsule or the Earth.

Not like: not a painted or rendered fantasy scene; no pirates, no treasure chest, no gold coins, no skulls, no candles, no dramatic glow and no clutter. Not a harbour and no boats. Not cut paper or kraft card. Not woodcut, linocut, risograph or any ink printmaking texture. Not cel-shaded outlined cartoon or anime. Not a 3D render. Not a stock vector illustration with gradients. Not a realistic space photograph or a science fiction film render. Original design only. Fresh composition from this text only; no input or reference images.
