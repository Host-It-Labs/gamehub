Use case: ui-mockup. Create one complete in-game screen of a desktop browser card game, concept art for a new art direction of a pirate-themed trick-taking game called Nox, played at night. Landscape 1536x1024 PNG, largest native landscape size, highest quality, no watermark.

Style, direction CHART: confident mid-century modernist flat graphic design, like a bold 1950s pictorial map poster, a treasure map drawn by a great graphic designer. Everything is built from a small number of large, clean, flat shapes with crisp edges: rectangles, circles, half circles, triangles, crescents and long parallel stripes, arranged on a clear grid with generous calm areas. Flat colour only: no brush texture, no paper grain, no ink texture, no painterly shading, no soft gradients, no fine detail. Depth comes only from overlapping flat shapes, flat offset shadows and one translucent flat shape of light. The pirate motifs (skulls, crossbones, cutlasses, flags, ships, treasure) are drawn as bold, witty, simplified graphic symbols, the way a great poster designer would draw them, never as detailed illustration. It looks designed, deliberate and full of swagger yet calm, like a real printed poster by a great graphic designer, never like a digital painting. Palette, strictly limited: deep navy sea and a darker blue-black, teal, warm cream, palm green, black, and treasure red as the accent.

World: a pirate treasure map at night, the whole screen one big flat pictorial chart of a pirate sea seen straight from above. The play surface is the calm open sea at the centre of the chart: one large rectangle of deep navy, with a slim cream line around it a little inside its edge; the rim band outside that line is a calm, continuous frame of flat cream and red rope pattern, like the border of an inset on the map. Around it, filling the picture to every edge: the rest of the sea in teal and navy with rows of small flat cream wave marks; islands as bold flat cream and green shapes with tiny palm stars, one with a smoking volcano cone; a pirate galleon drawn as a bold black silhouette with cream sails and a tiny skull flag; a sea serpent drawn as three looping teal arches with a head; a long dotted red route curving across the chart to a big red X on an island at the upper right; a bold flat compass rose, a sharp eight-pointed star, at the upper left; a skull island shaped like a flat cream skull; a whirlpool drawn as a flat spiral; a crescent moon and a few star dots in the top corner. Light: the moon at the upper left is the single light source; its light is one long translucent cream wedge falling across the upper left of the chart, and every flat shadow falls toward the lower right.

Layout of the game screen, drawn in this style. The play area is a wide rectangle, face-on and perfectly flat in true plan view, spanning about the middle 72 percent of the image width and running from about 13 percent to about 70 percent of the image height; the scenery surrounds it on every side and fills the picture to every edge. Nothing stands inside the play area: it is one calm, even surface. Around the play area runs a calm, continuous rim band, empty so that name tags can sit on it anywhere. Four players sit around the table, each shown only as a compact horizontal pill-shaped seat tag lying flat on the rim band, its text upright and horizontal: a small circular avatar showing one simple flat pirate-flag emblem (a skull, crossed swords, an hourglass or a heart pierced by a sword, one per player), a short name and a small score number. The viewer's tag lies on the bottom rim, clearly left of centre, about one third of the way along from the left end; the others lie on the middle of the left rim, the middle of the top rim and the middle of the right rim. The top rim tag has a bright ring in the accent colour around its avatar, showing that it is that player's turn. Just inside the rim, four small flat chevron arrows, one at the middle of each side, chase each other clockwise around the table: the one on the top side points right, the one on the right side points down, the one on the bottom side points left and the one on the left side points up. In the middle of the play area lies the current trick: three face-up cards lying flat and straight, slightly apart, each one nudged toward the player who played it (left, top and right), each with a flat offset shadow that falls away from the light. No chairs, no stools, no benches, no people, no pirates and no hands anywhere.

Cards: crisp cream-white cards with small rounded corners and a thin inner border, each with one large bold numeral at the top left in a heavy modernist sans serif and one large flat geometric suit shape in the centre. Five suits, each with its own colour: spades black, hearts treasure red, clubs teal, diamonds palm green, and a fifth suit, Storm, whose card face is split diagonally into treasure red and blue-black with a bold cream lightning bolt. The storm cards are the dangerous ones.

The hand dock at the bottom, below the play area: a long floating tray with rounded ends in flat blue-black with a thin cream rope edge, about 60 percent of the image width, resting just above the bottom edge, holding ten overlapping face-up cards in one straight row, each overlapping the next so that its numeral and suit stay visible, one of them a storm card; one card is lifted clear above the others and brought to the front, selected. At the left end of the tray: one flat die drawn as a cream rounded square showing a heart suit shape, and beside it two small round flat tokens, one with a shield shape and the number 2, one with a grappling hook, a straight shaft ending in four curved claws with a ring at the top. At the right end of the tray: a flat rounded rectangular button in the accent colour with the single word Play, and next to it a small round cream button with a curved undo arrow.

Navigation drawn on top, and nothing else: top-left corner, a small round cream button with a left arrow, and beside it the word Nox in a bold display typeface in the spirit of the poster. Top-right corner, one small round cream menu button with three short horizontal lines. Top centre, one small cream pill showing 2/4. No other interface, no status bar, no captions, no helper text.

Text: the only words in the image are Nox and Play. The only other characters are the card numerals from 1 to 10, the round count 2/4, the small score numbers and the four short names on the seat tags: You, Rook, Static and Moth. No other letters, words, labels, logos, brand names or watermark anywhere, no place names, no map labels, no compass letters, no scale bar and no cartouche text on the map.

Not like: not a painted or rendered fantasy pirate illustration, no digital painting, no realistic lighting, no glow effects, no piles of detailed loot and no clutter. Not cut paper or kraft card. Not woodcut, linocut, risograph or any ink printmaking texture. Not cel-shaded outlined cartoon or anime. Not a 3D render. Not a stock vector illustration with gradients. No existing film, franchise or brand. Not an old parchment map with stains or burnt edges. Original design only. Fresh composition from this text only; no input or reference images.
