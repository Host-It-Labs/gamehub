Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-bleed background plate for the web puzzle game Folio, landscape 1536x1024. Interface panels will be placed on top of it, so it is scenery only.
Primary request: A wide, calm panorama of a folded-newspaper mountain world. Bold paper mountains with crisp folded edges rise along the lower third and the far left and right edges; a winding cream paper trail climbs from the bottom centre and disappears between distant peaks. A low warm sun with a few radiating crayon rays sits near the upper right. Small cobalt pine trees made of torn paper dot the foothills. A few faint newsprint columns and a subtle crossword pattern are printed on some mountain faces like texture.
Style/medium: Distinctive mid-century editorial risograph illustration with assured flat shapes, crayon grain, uneven printed ink, visible paper fibers and folded paper surfaces. Warm, witty, intelligent; polished art direction, never generic fantasy.
Color palette: Warm butter-yellow paper sky, deep cobalt ink mountains, muted sage and ochre foothills, selective vermilion accents only on the sun and one tiny flag on the highest peak.
Composition/framing: Edge-to-edge. The central 60 percent of the width and the upper-middle 55 percent of the height must be quiet: mostly plain grainy butter-yellow paper sky with only faint distant peaks, so text laid over it stays readable. Detail concentrates at the bottom band (about the lowest 30 percent) and the outer 18 percent on each side. Soft low contrast in the quiet zone.
Constraints: No text, no letters, no numbers, no words, no logos, no title, no interface, no panels, no people, no frame, no border, no watermark.
Avoid: glossy 3D, gradients, photorealism, stock vector icons, tiny fussy detail, busy centre.
