# Folio cover v1

Dedicated full-bleed square artwork generated on 22 September 2026 through the
built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool, from the [exact written prompt](cover-v1.prompt.txt).
No prior covers or reference images were inspected or supplied.

The approved asset review checks were performed on the actual 1254 × 1254 source
and the [160px thumbnail](cover-thumbnail-160.png): **Folio** is spelled correctly
and appears once; its large cobalt typography remains immediately readable at
thumbnail size. The winding newspaper trail, paper mountains, sunrise, letter
tiles, number blocks, pencil and crossword retain a cohesive editorial print
identity. Artwork covers all square edges, without a frame or mockup. Scenery at
the edges is intentionally full bleed. No retouching or title replacement was
performed.

- Original source: `sources/cover-v1.png`
- Native WebP: `public/art/optimized/box-folio-blind-v1.webp` (1254 × 1254)
- Responsive sizes: `-320.webp`, `-640.webp`, `-960.webp`
- Companion spine: `-spine.webp` (60 × 960, cropped from the outer left texture)
- Reproducible export: `export.mjs`, run from the repository root
- Provenance and source checksum: `provenance.json`
- Ready-to-merge shared preview entry: `preview-entry.json`

All production assets are persisted in the repository. The export uses the
archived source when it exists, so future exports do not depend on the generation
cache. Shared registries and manifests were not changed by the asset task.
Browser, rendered shelf, and touch checks were not performed.
