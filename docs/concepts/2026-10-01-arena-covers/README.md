# Arena covers v5: the loading screen's cartoon style (1 October 2026)

The library box and the setup lid were still the dark, realistic "Collector's
Diorama" v4 covers, while the loading screen (concepts/2026-09-29-arena-quest)
is bright stylised cartoon key art. William asked for covers that look like the
game. Each prompt reuses the loading screen's style paragraph verbatim, plus one
added sentence ruling out a dark or dusk scene. Two variants per image, one
image per run, `codex exec -m gpt-6-luna -c model_reasoning_effort="low"`
(Codex CLI 0.157.1) through `gen.sh`, which accepts a copy only if the run's own
session folder holds exactly one PNG with the same hash. All four passed.

| image | file | size | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| square a | `assets/arena/concepts/box-arena-square-v5-a.png` | 1254² | **picked**: clean title, four big characters, reads at thumbnail size |
| square b | `assets/arena/concepts/box-arena-square-v5-b.png` | 1254² | good; a crown and ribbons around the title make it busier |
| wide a | `assets/arena/concepts/box-arena-wide-v5-a.png` | 1536×1024 | **picked**: in the 3:1 cut (y 256–768) every character, both bridges and the title stay whole |

`node scripts/arena-assets.mjs --only=covers` writes the served files
(`box-arena-blind-v2*`, `box-arena-wide-v2*`).

Clean hand-painted cartoon rendering with soft cel-like shading in two or three tones per colour, a crisp dark outline feel around the forms, simple smooth colour areas and only the key details, bright highlights on edges, saturated but tasteful warm colour, warm late-afternoon sunlight from the upper left. Not photoreal, not a glossy 3D render, not a detailed miniature, not a toy, not realistic anatomy, no photographic texture, no film grain, no fine micro detail, no clutter; also not childlike toddler art, not baby or chibi mascots: confident, characterful and a little heroic or funny. Not a dark or moody scene, not a dusk or night sky, not stone-grey realism. Original design, not any existing game, film, brand or franchise. Scene: a bright, warm cartoon valley battlefield seen from a gentle high angle: rolling green grass, a sparkling blue river crossing the lower middle of the picture with a chunky stone arch bridge in the centre, a squat blue castle tower with a cobalt-blue roof and a blank cobalt banner at the left edge and a matching squat red castle tower with a red roof and a blank red banner at the right edge, clumps of round cartoon trees, a bright warm sky with big soft clouds. Few big characters so the cover reads at thumbnail size, meeting at the bridge in a burst of energy: from the left, the blue team, a samurai in chunky navy-blue lacquered armour with a big golden horned crest swinging a katana and a bearded wizard in a cobalt-blue robe and tall pointed cobalt hat raising a staff crowned by a glowing orange crystal; from the right, the red team, a big cartoon tyrannosaurus rex with a red hide roaring with its jaw open and a big broad viking with a plain round iron helmet with a nose guard (no horns), a braided ginger beard and a long two-handed axe; a red First World War biplane swooping overhead at the upper right and a cobalt-and-cream striped hot-air balloon at the upper left, both small and clear of the title. Every character is distinct, readable and full of energy, with lively cartoon faces. Exact sole text: "Arena" (capital A, lowercase r e n a). Render it yourself as big chunky custom cartoon lettering in warm gold with a thick dark outline and a soft warm glow around it, integrated into the composition, unmistakably readable. The title sits across the upper part of the picture over the sky, dominating the composition, every stroke inside the central area from 10% to 90% of the width and between about 8% and 32% down from the top edge, with clear breathing room; it is unmistakably readable at 160px thumbnail size. No other text, letters, numbers or glyphs anywhere; banners, shields and flags are blank or carry a simple non-letter shape. No border, no mat, no frame, no vignette, no gray padding, no box mockup, no perspective packaging, no UI, no game screenshot, no buttons, no health bars, no counters, no icons. Artwork fills all four edges. Fresh composition from this text only; no input or reference images.
