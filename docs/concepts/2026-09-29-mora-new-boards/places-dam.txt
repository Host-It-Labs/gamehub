World: "Glacier Dam". THE SIX PLACES AND THEIR PADS. Every pad is an empty flat cream cardstock piece seen almost face-on, large and readable, clearly separated from its neighbours, each pad the same size; there are no look-alike cream stones, discs or tiles anywhere else. Square pads are soft-cornered squares; round pads are circles. Each place stands apart on its own ground with its own material, so the six places read at a glance.
1. Reservoir: the frozen lake behind the dam, a flat sheet of pale blue paper ice with cracks and a few snow patches. Exactly SIX SQUARE pads on the ice, in two rows of three.
2. Turbine hall: the roofless old turbine hall at the foot of the dam, a flat concrete floor with two big rusted turbine wheels against the back wall. Exactly FIVE SQUARE pads on the floor, a row of three above a row of two.
3. Cable car: the old cable car station, a small concrete platform under a steel frame, with the faded red cabin hanging open beside it. Exactly FOUR SQUARE pads on the platform floor, in a 2 x 2 block.
4. Spillway: the wide stepped spillway on the face of the dam, a staircase of broad flat concrete steps with a thin frozen waterfall of paper icicles at one side. Exactly FIVE ROUND pads, one on each of five steps, in one straight horizontal line along the steps, the gap between pads no wider than half a pad.
5. Ice cave: the low mouth of a blue glacier ice cave in the mountainside, with a wide flat floor of packed snow in front of it. Exactly FIVE ROUND pads on that snow floor, a row of three above a row of two, all fully visible and not hidden by the ice arch.
6. Summit hut: a small squat stone mountain hut with a steep snowy roof, on a rocky knoll, with a flat wooden deck at its front. Exactly THREE ROUND pads in one row on that deck, in front of the hut, never covered by it.
Exactly twenty-eight empty cream pads in total: 6 + 5 + 4 + 5 + 5 + 3. Directly below every pad group, a narrow strip of plain snow or plain concrete about half a pad tall for a text label. A small empty patch of plain snow, about the size of one pad, in the middle of the dam for releasing creatures. Little paper paths of flat stepping stones and small bridges join every place. No text, letters, numbers, icons, animals, people, cards, frame or interface. The picture runs edge to edge with no border or table.
