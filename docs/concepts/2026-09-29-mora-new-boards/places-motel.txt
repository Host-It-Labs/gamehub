World: "Sunset Motel". THE SIX PLACES AND THEIR PADS. Every pad is an empty flat cream cardstock piece seen almost face-on, large and readable, clearly separated from its neighbours, each pad the same size; there are no look-alike cream stones, discs or tiles anywhere else. Square pads are soft-cornered squares; round pads are circles. Each place stands apart on its own ground with its own material, so the six places read at a glance.
1. Pool: the motel's empty swimming pool, dry and half filled with drifted sand, with a pale turquoise tiled rim and a rusted ladder. Exactly FIVE SQUARE pads on the sandy pool floor, a row of three above a row of two.
2. Rooms: the long low row of motel rooms with faded coral doors, their roof partly fallen in, cacti growing out of the rooms. Exactly FOUR SQUARE pads on the cracked concrete walkway in front of the doors, in a 2 x 2 block.
3. Garage: the old gas station with its flat canopy on two posts, a rusted pump and the rusted old car beside it. Exactly THREE SQUARE pads in one row on the oil-stained concrete under the canopy.
4. Road: a straight stretch of the old cracked highway disappearing under the sand, with faded dashed centre lines. Exactly FIVE ROUND pads spaced evenly along the centre line in one straight horizontal line, the gap between pads no wider than half a pad.
5. Diner: the roofless little diner with a black and white checkered floor, a curved counter and round stools pushed against the wall. Exactly FOUR ROUND pads on the checkered floor in a 2 x 2 block.
6. Sign: the foot of the old motel sign, a short rusted sign post with an arrow-shaped sign board, standing on a small round concrete platform. Exactly TWO ROUND pads side by side on that platform, in front of the post, never covered by it.
Exactly twenty-three empty cream pads in total: 5 + 4 + 3 + 5 + 4 + 2. Directly below every pad group, a narrow strip of plain sand or plain concrete about half a pad tall for a text label. A small empty patch of plain sand, about the size of one pad, in the middle of the motel for releasing creatures. Little paper paths of flat stepping stones join every place. No text, letters, numbers, icons, animals, people, cards, frame or interface. The picture runs edge to edge with no border or table.
