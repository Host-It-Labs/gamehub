# Puzzles, Rota and Diner covers, 9 October 2026

Fresh square library covers and wide (3:1) setup-lid covers for three games:

- **Puzzles** (`folio`, renamed from Folio; ID and `/folio` route unchanged): the
  ten daily puzzles on one endless branching trail, kept in Folio's vintage
  newspaper puzzle-page look (cobalt on butter yellow, vermilion).
- **Rota**: the old cover was the surveyor's cyanotype blueprint; the game is
  now a 3D mill town (DESIGN.md "Rota as a mill town" and later sections), so
  the cover shows that town at golden hour turning to dusk.
- **Diner**: the diner car (cab-over truck towing a trailer whose side is the
  kitchen hatch) at a desert lot at dusk, with a slim, graceful, softly
  effeminate young man as the server, on roller skates.

## Generation

Text-only generations, one image per Codex invocation, two variants (`-a`,
`-b`) per image, six or seven runs at a time through `batch.sh` → `gen.sh`.
No reference images, no edits. Model `gpt-6-luna` (newest Luna from
`.claude/skills/artwork/newest-luna.mjs`), low reasoning effort, codex-cli
0.161.0, stdin closed. `gen.sh` requires exactly one PNG in the run's own
`~/.codex/generated_images/<session id>/` folder and checks the saved copy by
sha256 (three runs first copied another run's image; the wrapper replaced
each copy with the run's own PNG). Logs are in `logs/`.

The prompt for both variants of an image is the file in `prompts/` (exact
text below). The chosen PNGs are kept in `sources/`; the other variants were
moved to the art archive beside the repo
(`../gamehub-art-archive/docs/concepts/2026-10-09-puzzles-rota-diner-covers/`).

Delivery: `node scripts/optimize-box-covers.mjs folio diner`,
`node scripts/optimize-wide-covers.mjs folio diner` and
`node scripts/optimize-rota-art.mjs` write `box-puzzles-blind-v1`,
`box-puzzles-wide-v1`, `box-rota-blind-v2`, `box-rota-wide-v2`,
`box-diner-blind-v3` and `box-diner-wide-v3` (with `-320`, `-640`, `-spine`
and `-768` siblings) into `public/art/optimized/`.
