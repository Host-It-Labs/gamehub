# Yata: a phone counter (25 September 2026)

William: on phones the table felt too small, with too much random content at the top (the tall plate's cook's band: steamer, wok, bottles under the badges). The bottom section (dock with extensions, two rows of tickets) was the right size.

The tall plate's counter is 2:1, so on a 390 px phone it is width-bound at about 390 × 190 px however much height the stage has. The fix is a third plate, for portrait phones only: a near-square counter placed high, only the awning edge and lantern glow above it, the plank floor below for the dock and tickets. Tablets and squarish windows keep the tall plate.

Every image is a fresh generation from its prompt text; no image was opened while writing prompts. Two variants per brief, launched in parallel (`gen.sh`, Codex `$imagegen`, `-m gpt-6-astra`, stdin closed: the first two runs hung on "Reading additional input from stdin" and were killed, logs kept as `logs/phone-*-hung-1.log`).

## Runtime rule

`yataPhonePlateFits` (lib/games/table-world.ts) compares the counter area each plate gets on the measured stage and picks the bigger one, so short phones (375 × 667, 360 × 780: a tall dock over a wide, shallow stage) keep the wide counter. The phone plate's stage starts 12 px higher (table-worlds.css) because its counter starts 11.5% down the picture; the art then reaches every edge at the cover scale.

## Attempts

Counter including its cream rim, sampled across the rim on three rows and columns (median). Brief target bands t / b / l / r ≈ 0.13 / 0.50 / 0.17 / 0.17; the hard requirement is the width: at most about 0.68 of the picture, or a 390 × 844 or 360 × 780 phone cannot be covered at the height-cover scale.
