# Mora paper creatures v1

Rebuild the twelve delivered WebP sprites from repository source atlases with `node scripts/prepare-mora-paper-creatures.mjs`. The script retains generated alpha without masking, applies the exact measured inclusive bounds with two pixels of edge padding, fits each creature within 460 × 460, and centers it on a transparent 512 × 512 canvas. Encoding uses WebP quality 90 and alpha quality 100. A rebuild was verified byte-identical to the delivered sprites using the installed Sharp version.

Generated 2026-09-16 with the built-in image_gen tool. Fresh artwork, using public/art/observatory-diorama-v2.png solely as tactile tree material reference. No prior creature art was edited. Original generated PNG atlases are retained in public/art; WebP sprites retain alpha and are independently cropped to creature bounds then fit inside 512 × 512 with transparent padding.

Six separate full-body creatures, one centered inside each cell with ample transparent padding, no overlaps. Every animal unmistakably constructed from stacked hand-cut fibrous cardstock and torn handmade paper, many thin relief layers, matte pulp texture, rough paper edges, tiny shadows between layers, restrained natural colors, sculptural three-quarter view. Same tangible layered paper material as the reference tree foliage and bark. Elegant readable animal silhouettes, no anthropomorphic clothing, no electronics. Genuine transparent background and alpha, no ground, no cast ground shadow, no pedestal or base, no frame, no letters, no captions, no cell lines, no glossy plastic, no smooth clay. Each creature occupies 70 percent of its cell and remains entirely inside it.
