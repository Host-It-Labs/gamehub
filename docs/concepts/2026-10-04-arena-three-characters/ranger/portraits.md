built-in imagegen tool, from written briefs only. No images were supplied to
either generation. Both sources and exact prompts remain preserved.

The author opened the complete 1254-square outputs and a 120-pixel comparison
of the current v7-b, v10-a and v10-b. v10-a is rejected and preserved: its face
leans realistic and its shirt lacks the original blue contrast. v10-b keeps
clear adult facial planes and a calm smirk, brown felt hat, substantial sand
duster, royal-blue shirt/kerchief, dark vest and a complete two-hand walnut/
brass lever-rifle firing action. The closed fingers wrap the grips coherently.

The parent independently opened v10-b and the thumbnail comparison and
accepted v10-b for integration: hat, face, blue shirt, sand coat, rifle and
brass remain readable; painted planes fit the deck. This is root visual
review, not user acceptance. No manifest or card-art mapping was changed by
the author. Final integration remains root-owned.
