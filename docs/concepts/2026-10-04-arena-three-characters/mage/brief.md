## Character and construction

A dignified weathered old traveller, focused rather than comical. Sculpt a
gaunt adult face with connected brow, cheek, hooked nose and jaw planes, inset
eyes and lids, restrained forehead/eye furrows and a visible compressed mouth.
White brows, side hair and the long beard are authored separated locks with
combed ridges, not a smooth grey beard cone or random strands. The tall cobalt
felt hat bends under its own weight, has an irregular turned brim and modest
muted-gold embroidered band. Its current complete silhouette is protected.

The cobalt robe is heavyweight tailored cloth: gathered vertical skirt folds,
diagonal pull beneath the belt, compressed elbow folds, hanging sleeve bell,
dark lining and restrained embroidered trim. A charcoal travelling mantle
rests over the shoulders and flows behind in two weighted links. Cloth needs
different roughness and surface structure from weathered skin, silver hair,
worn leather, knotted wood, aged silver/gold and restrained amber crystal.
Surface detail stays subordinate to the face and garment silhouette.

The right hand closes four separate curled fingers and a thumb around the
knotted staff. Preserve the grip pivot. The left palm cups the small flame,
with separate fingers, thenar volume and understated nails. Hands remain
adult-sized. Sleeves use continuous weighted topology through the shoulder
and elbow rather than visible elbow balls or disconnected rigid cuts.

The robe hem reveals boots stepping beneath it. Author real knee and ankle
pivots, small shin masses hidden by the robe and planted leather soles. Keep
the inherited hip/waist/shoulder/wrist/staff positions for existing contact
logic. Preserve torso, head, all gait joints, arms/forearms, weapon, offhand,
extra/extra2, cape/cape2, hat, beard, eye/pupil/brow/mouth controls.

## Motion and cameras

The Wizard gathers weight before a cast, shifts forward with the release and
settles. The staff remains in the closed hand over its full turn; the sleeve
elbow deforms visibly in a representative overhead pose. Existing distinctive
staff planting, flame toss, peering and travelling motions remain unless the
new joint structure exposes a defect. Walking needs actual alternating boot
steps, level soles and no motion concealed by a floor-length hem.

Review front, side, rear and three-quarter neutral clay, face and both hands,
fabric/hat/staff close-ups, a clear bent-arm cast, and elevated intended game
cameras (portrait 63 degrees and landscape 55 degrees). Review the actual
export in Arena separately. Source acceptance is not user approval.
