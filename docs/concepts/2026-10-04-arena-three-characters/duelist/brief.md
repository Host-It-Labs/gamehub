# Samurai: authored anatomy and lacquer armour

Scope: large 3D artwork rework of Arena's existing `duelist` only. No gameplay change, global scale change, or shared build/manifest change is authorized for this author. Root owns integration and browser review.

## Character

A mature adult warrior, compact and poised, with a deliberate open-faced kabuto silhouette. The original broad gold kuwagata, cobalt lacquer, pale silk lacing, charcoal hakama, two-handed katana and back sashimono make the identity immediately readable. Keep decoration subordinate to his face, armour planes and weapon; replace swollen primitive rhythms with coherent authored forms. His expression is focused rather than enraged, with a strong chin, cheek planes, brow sockets, shaped nostrils, thin lips and restrained groomed facial hair.

## Structure

Retain feet at Z=0, +Y forward, waist pivot `(0,0,.64)`, shoulders `(+/-.215,0,1)`, neck `(0,0,1.035)`, hips `(+/-.095,0,.60)` and original opposite-foot stance. The two fists retain the original hilt axis and centres; both shoulders stay on the same X axis so common X rotation preserves grip. Sword length, edge, guard origin and tip keep the source contract. Do not reduce native figure size to hide contact problems.

Author a connected dark under-robe across the sloping shoulders and each bent arm, with local elbow compression and forearm taper. Rigid armour plates sit over the robe and articulate at their existing named joints. The hakama must have meaningful lengthwise pleats and gathered tension above the calf wraps, with connected deforming knee/ankle coverage under overlapping armour. Closed fingers have knuckle/pad hierarchy, visible finger divisions and a continuous thumb web; the wrist tapers into the forearm and both hands contact the hilt.

## Finish

Cobalt lacquer has broad controlled reflections, dark plate recesses and layered lips. Gold is restricted to shaped crest, fittings and a few armour rims. Woven silk cords carry restrained twist, and charcoal cloth folds follow tension rather than repeating inflated ridges. Skin uses the dedicated matte skin family; hair carries combed relief. Preserve exact old sources and all source versions, then save a detailed editable Blender master. Do not decimate until root has inspected the source.
